shim `bc` that emits the distro include/generated/timeconst.h was put on PATH (as build-xe.sh does);

    make -j16 modules_prepare
    ln -sfn /usr/lib/modules/7.2.9-arch1-1/build/vmlinux vmlinux   # BTF base for CONFIG_DEBUG_INFO_BTF_MODULES

build-xe.sh's intree mode; `-w` makes unresolved symbols warnings.)
